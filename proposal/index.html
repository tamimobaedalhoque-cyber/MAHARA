03 Three versions

Overview

Three design versions

Version 01

Timber slats & gold

A curved wall of oak slats wraps the stand with the mark cut in backlit gold. Backlit campaign graphic in a gold frame, white reception, brochure kiosk, glass meeting room.

Curved slat wall · gold lightbox · brochure kiosk
Version 02

White, oak & steel

A tighter footprint with a backlit services panel, brushed stainless reception, white sofas and a glass meeting room. A portrait LED screen on the end column faces the aisle.

Services lightbox · steel reception · LED column
Version 03

Travertine curve

A continuous travertine wall carved by one warm LED line. The mark sits large on the stone; a white backlit panel carries the services. Lounge, hospitality ledge and glass meeting room.

Stone wall · LED line · white services panel

Version 03 · concept & materials

One wall, one line of light

The whole stand is one sweeping travertine surface. A single LED groove runs along it, steps down behind the lounge and lifts again at the meeting room. The calligraphic mark sits large on the stone; a white backlit panel carries the four services.
